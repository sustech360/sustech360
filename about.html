About

A publication about energy, materials and sustainability, written by the people doing the work.

What we publish

Research highlights, explanations of technology, industry analysis and opinion — across energy, energy storage, materials, sustainability and the policy around them. Every article states which audience it is written for: a general reader, a technical reader outside the speciality, or a specialist.

Who writes here

Contribution is by invitation. We approach researchers, engineers and industry practitioners directly; there is no open submission form, and there is no way to register as an author. That keeps the standard predictable and the review load honest.

How an article gets published

An invited author writes and submits. An editor takes it on and asks a reviewer with relevant expertise to read it. Revisions go back to the author. Once the editors are satisfied, the article is prepared for publication and approved by the supervising editor before it goes live.

Published articles are never edited in place. A correction or update creates a new version, and the previous version stays on the record. If a revision is rejected, what readers already have is untouched.

Advertising and sponsorship

Advertising is labelled as advertising and sponsored material is labelled as sponsored. Advertisers have no influence over editorial decisions, and no advance sight of articles.

Contact

Write to the editors at the address on our contact page. Corrections are welcome and are acted on quickly.
