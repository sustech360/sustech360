Privacy

Written to be read, not to be complied with.

Reading this site

The pages are static files served from a content delivery network. Reading an article does not create an account, and we do not ask you to identify yourself.

What is measured

We collect anonymous performance measurements from your browser — how long the page took to appear, how much the layout moved, how quickly it responded — along with the type of page and whether your screen is narrow. No identifiers, no reading history, nothing that distinguishes you from anyone else reading the same page.

If advertising is running, we count how many times a creative was displayed and clicked. The count is attached to the advertisement, not to you.

If site analytics are enabled, they are noted here with the name of the provider before they are switched on.

Settings you change

Theme, text size and saved articles are stored in your own browser and never sent anywhere.

The newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address and the date you confirmed it. You are only added after clicking a confirmation link, every email carries a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing removes you immediately. We do not sell, rent or share the list.

Authors and reviewers

People who write or review for us have accounts holding their name, institution, email and the work they have done here. Author profiles show only what the author chose to make public.

Asking us to remove something

Write to the editors and we will remove what we can and explain what we cannot. Published articles stay on the record — that is what publication means — but personal details that do not belong there can be taken out.

Advertising

We show advertising from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to serve advertisements and to measure whether they were seen. This may include advertising based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

You can review and change what Google uses at My Ad Center, and opt out of personalised advertising entirely at Google Ads Settings. Details of how Google uses data from sites that show its advertising are at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising on this site is always labelled, either as Advertisement or as Sponsored. Advertisers have no say in what we publish, and no advance sight of it.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom

Personalised advertising there requires your consent, collected through a certified consent platform. Until one is in place on this site, advertising shown to readers in those regions is non-personalised.
